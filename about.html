The Atelier

From a small studio to thousands of homes

Borka Mart was born in 2014 at a single cutting table in Dhanmondi. Our founder couldn't find a borka that was modest, breathable in our climate, and truly beautiful — so she began designing her own.

Today our atelier is home to a family of pattern-makers, embroiderers and finishers. Every design is still sketched, sampled and perfected in-house before it reaches you.
